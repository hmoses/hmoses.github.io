UX writing approach · illustrative examples

Writing for moments that matter

For host experiences, clear content should explain next steps, set expectations, reduce ambiguity, and give people agency—especially around complex or consequential actions.

ClaritySet expectations before action

“Review your listing details before publishing. You can make changes later.”

Illustrative sample, not Airbnb product copy.
ConfidenceExplain what happens next

“Your updates are saved. Guests will see the new information when it's published.”

Illustrative sample, not Airbnb product copy.

“We couldn't save this change. Check your connection and try again.”
