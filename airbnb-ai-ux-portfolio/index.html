Case study · Poly-Glot

UX writing for multi-model AI

Challenge. Multiple AI providers and prompt workflows introduce unfamiliar concepts, varying behaviors, and opportunities for user confusion.

Approach. Develop clear actions, onboarding messages, comparison guidance, and reusable terminology. Treat interface copy, help, and subscription language as one connected experience.

Content considerations

  • Help users understand what an action will do before they take it.
  • Keep UI labels concise and consistent across 38 localized interfaces, including RTL languages.
  • Review generated responses and refine prompts with a human-in-the-loop process.
  • Use product feedback and analytics to identify and address friction.

Case study · RAG / structured writing

Make content findable for humans and AI

Challenge. Inconsistent documentation structure makes technical guidance harder to navigate, reuse, and retrieve in RAG systems.

Approach. Structure Markdown/MDX using meaningful headings, metadata, coherent sections, chunking, and summaries. The same content architecture supports clear human reading and machine retrieval.

Why it matters for AI UX

  • Clear source structure helps AI systems retrieve relevant context.
  • Consistent terminology supports understandable generated answers.
  • Reviewable source material helps people assess AI output quality.
